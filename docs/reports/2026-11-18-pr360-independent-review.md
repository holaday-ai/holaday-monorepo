Exercise delayed final target validation across a natural reconnect with the welcome held back. Check the browser effect count, not just the RPC response. The old bearer predicate existed at base. 2. **[P1] Saved screenshots can include content from an ungranted frame.** `packages/browser-driver/src/task-screenshot.ts:29` iterates every frame and evaluates the sensitive-field collector in it. It then masks controls and their mirrored values, leaving ordinary text and graphics in the JPEG (`:36–45`). This diverges from the selected-target observer's exact-origin grant and express rule to skip ungranted documents (`packages/browser-driver/src/selected-target.ts:711–744`). On a permitted page embedding an authenticated third-party frame, that frame's ordinary account text or graphics become part of the saved file under the top-level grant. Use the existing frame-grant rules, accounting for inherited origins. Fail capture when frame authority cannot be established, or conceal the entire excluded frame from the parent without entering it. Cover an otherwise valid top-level grant with a foreign child and verify the child content cannot be read or delivered. This is a new capture path.
